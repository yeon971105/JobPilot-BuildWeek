# JobPilot — Know why a job fits

Most job tools show a score before they show a reason. JobPilot turns a fictional role and candidate evidence into an inspectable career decision: required versus preferred qualifications, relevant experience, practical constraints, exact point allocation, and a downloadable Score Receipt. The redesigned Overview shows the decision immediately, while Evidence, Experience, and Score Proof keep full transparency one click away.

Gemma 4 12B is the primary semantic model. Deterministic code—not a model—calculates AI Fit V2.2, reconciles 100 visible points, keeps hidden adjustments at zero, and hashes the receipt. GPT-5.6 Terra is optional heavy reasoning for explicit strategy and critique; the no-key public demo uses clearly labeled prepared strategy output, and GPT-5.6 never owns the score.

The public judge experience needs no login, key, local model, or personal data. It uses Demo Candidate A, six fictional roles, prepared Gemma analyses, and a browser-local tracker. The local edition adds real PDF, DOCX, and TXT resume onboarding: in-memory parsing, editable profile review, work preferences, live loopback Gemma matching, deterministic scoring, and no cloud resume transmission.

JP-BW6 reduced the default Alder detail from 1,932 to 268 visible words and from 13,477 to 1,689 pixels without deleting proof. Technical JSON moved into a keyboard-safe modal, provider provenance became explicit, the strategy became a focused action, and desktop through 320px views were recertified.

The Fit Score is an evidence-based ranking score, not a hiring probability. Independent hiring-outcome calibration is not yet available. JobPilot never submits an application.
