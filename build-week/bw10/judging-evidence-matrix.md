# JP-BW10 official judging evidence matrix

The four official criteria are treated as equal-weight evidence categories. No judge score is estimated. Every entry in the companion JSON records the claim, route, test, screenshot reference, artifact, current weakness, and exact clearance condition.

## Technological implementation

- Exact micro-point AI Fit V2.2 arithmetic with no hidden adjustments.
- Independently reproducible Score Receipts across web, API, and CLI, including tamper failure.
- Local `gemma4:12b`, four prepared `gpt-5.6-sol` reviews, and zero OpenAI API runtime.
- Local-only private resume isolation and a fail-closed public resume route.
- Frozen coverage lineage, destination trust states, tests, and the Codex defect timeline.

## Design

- Application-fatigue Golden Path and Today's Shortlist.
- Concise Job Detail with profile context and progressive evidence disclosure.
- Nearby discovery, sorting, filters, and three-role comparison.
- Honest original-destination language, responsive QA, keyboard access, visible focus, and 44px controls.

## Potential impact

- A focused response to application fatigue and fragmented decision work.
- Nearby roles, private resume-aware prioritization, and a direct employer path.
- Frozen production acquisition proof with exact processed-portfolio limits.
- An authentic paired Decision Utility study pipeline. The study is `READY_NOT_RUN` with 0 participants, so the dry run is not impact evidence and no public metric is published.

## Quality of the idea

- Official-source discovery plus local resume privacy and deterministic scoring.
- An independently verifiable decision receipt.
- Prepared GPT-5.6 critique without opaque live inference.
- No auto-apply; the human remains responsible for the decision and employer handoff.
