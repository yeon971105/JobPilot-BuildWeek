2. Show Landing and the focused For You shortlist.
3. Open the decision-first Job Detail.
4. Open the concise Application Strategy and explain prepared GPT-5.6 provenance.
5. Reveal grouped evidence only on demand.
6. Show visual Score Proof and independent receipt verification.
7. Open the fictional employer destination without submitting anything.
8. Show browser-local tracking.
9. Close with Gemma, deterministic scoring, Codex, zero API calls, privacy, and limitations.
