## Description

JobPilot turns synthetic jobs and candidate evidence into transparent career decisions. This 2:55 product RC demonstrates a concise role overview, progressive evidence and experience, deterministic Score Proof, a reproducible receipt, score sensitivity, private local PDF/DOCX/TXT onboarding, prepared application strategy, and Trust Lab.

Gemma 4 12B is the primary local semantic model. Deterministic AI Fit V2.2 calculates every score. GPT-5.6 Terra is optional heavy reasoning and is not live in the no-key demonstration. The Fit Score is not a hiring probability, and JobPilot never submits an application.
