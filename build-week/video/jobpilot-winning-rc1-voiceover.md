# JobPilot winning RC narration

Job search fatigue is not just too many listings. It is repeating the same searches, opening one posting at a time, comparing every requirement manually, and still wondering which application deserves the next hour. JobPilot turns that scattered work into one controlled decision flow.

The no-login demo begins with nearby roles from a processed portfolio of official and rights-eligible employer sources. Public roles and the candidate are synthetic. Location comes from the user’s configured profile, so the browser never requests device location and no geocoding or map service is called.

Best Match is deterministic and visible. Judges can switch to Nearest, Most Recent, or Highest Evidence Quality, then filter by work mode, seniority, priority, blocker state, radius, remote compatibility, and minimum fit. Distance and recency help prioritize attention, but neither becomes a hidden technical point adjustment.

Open Northstar and the decision is immediately legible: Fit Score, Evidence Quality, Apply Priority, blocker state, strongest matches, and truthful gaps. Every capability opens to the exact fictional job statement, candidate evidence ID, alias or equivalence, verifier status, uncertainty, and micro-point calculation. Relevant experience is calculated from dated month intervals, removes overlap once, and shows the requested years, relevance coefficients, and point impact.

The Score Receipt is independently verifiable. The bundled verifier reproduces canonical serialization, SHA-256 hashing, the exact one-hundred-million micro-point allocation, class caps, transfers, rounding, evidence IDs, and frozen source inputs. A modified score fails both hash and arithmetic checks. Verification makes zero external requests and needs no private resume.

Compare up to three roles side by side. Fit, evidence, required and preferred experience, gaps, blockers, distance, work mode, and posting date remain visible. There is no second aggregate formula. A prepared GPT-5.6 review can explain which of two strategies deserves attention first, but it creates no numeric score.

When the candidate chooses to continue, JobPilot opens the employer destination in a safe new tab. This fictional destination has no form, no hidden redirect, and a disabled application button. Returning never marks the role Applied. Only an explicit Save or Mark as Preparing action changes the browser-local tracker.

Prepared GPT-5.6 reviews add bounded strategy, critique, ambiguity analysis, and pairwise comparison from frozen synthetic evidence. They are clearly labeled prepared, not live. OpenAI API requests are zero, API cost is zero, and deterministic code remains the only owner of the score and receipt. Gemma 4 12B remains the primary semantic model, including a certified live loopback canary for the local edition.

The optional seven-step judge tour compresses discovery, prioritization, understanding, evidence inspection, verification, employer control, and tracking into about ninety seconds. It is skippable, restartable, keyboard accessible, screen-reader labeled, and mobile safe. JobPilot’s promise is simple: find the roles worth your time, know why they fit, and stay in control of every application.
