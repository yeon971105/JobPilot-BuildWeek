# JobPilot Final Product RC1 Voiceover

Job search fatigue is not just about finding openings. It is the repeated work of searching separate sites, rereading requirements, comparing a resume, and deciding whether another application deserves the next hour.

JobPilot starts with one promise: find the roles worth your time. The landing page explains the value before the implementation. For You then presents three focused decisions, with synthetic public roles and no account required.

Why these three is visible before the cards. Evidence fit, stated preferences, and freshness guide the shortlist through published deterministic ordering. Nearby roles come from the processed official-source portfolio in the production architecture; every public job shown here is synthetic.

Opening a role leads with the conclusion. Strong Fit, Apply Priority, Evidence Quality, blockers, and the employer destination are readable at a glance. Visual evidence cards separate supported capabilities from attention areas. Relevant experience and practical fit are clear without exposing point decimals. Private resume analysis remains local.

Exact evidence is still available on demand. Expand a capability to inspect the role quote, candidate quote, aliases, verifier status, uncertainty, and exact low, midpoint, and high proof. Progressive disclosure keeps rigor without overwhelming the first decision.

Score Proof turns the frozen one-hundred-point contract into visible allocation, contribution, and Evidence Quality bars. The exact Technical Ledger remains one disclosure away.

Every Score Receipt is independently verifiable. The verifier reproduces canonical hashing, micro-point arithmetic, caps, transfers, evidence identifiers, rounding, and bundled frozen inputs. Hidden adjustments remain zero.

When a role is worth pursuing, JobPilot opens the original employer destination deliberately. The public flow demonstrates that handoff with a fictional employer page. There is no form, resume transmission, redirect, or automatic submission.

Saving a role updates only browser-local planning state. The tracker supports Saved, Interested, Preparing, and Applied, with Undo after a move. Applied is a planning label only; JobPilot never auto-applies.

Gemma 4 12B is the primary semantic model in the local architecture. Deterministic code calculates every score. GPT-5.6 Sol review is prepared through Codex, not live. OpenAI API calls and cost are zero. Codex helped reproduce, repair, and regression-test real defects.

Fit Score is not a hiring probability, and no human-study result is claimed without authentic final participants. JobPilot connects discovery, evidence, proof, employer handoff, and tracking so you can spend less time searching job by job and more time applying to roles that fit.
