# JobPilot Build Week demo script

Target: 2:45, 1080p, English narration, no music.

1. **0:00–0:15 — Problem.** “Job seekers spend hours reading listings, then receive opaque AI scores. They still cannot tell which requirement mattered or why points were lost.”
2. **0:15–0:35 — Promise.** “JobPilot is an evidence-first assistant: know why a job fits before you apply.”
3. **0:35–1:00 — Browse.** Open the synthetic no-login catalog and call out remote, hybrid, and onsite examples.
4. **1:00–1:40 — Evidence.** Open Northstar Labs. Show required versus preferred requirements, candidate evidence IDs, work modes, travel, Core/Preferred/Nice buckets, Evidence Quality, and visible maximum 100.
5. **1:40–2:10 — Strategy.** Activate the live GPT-5.6 strategy only after the hosted configuration is verified. Show strongest evidence, truthful gaps, research questions, and the Apply/Review/Skip rationale. Do not show any private resume or API key.
6. **2:10–2:35 — Technology.** Explain deterministic scoring, GPT-5.6 Structured Outputs for strategy, the local Gemma privacy-first Beta mode, and Codex implementation work.
7. **2:35–2:45 — Impact.** “JobPilot helps people decide where to spend limited application time before they apply.”
