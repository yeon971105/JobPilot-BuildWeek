## Title

JobPilot — Know Why a Job Fits Before You Apply | OpenAI Build Week

## Description

JobPilot is an evidence-first AI job search assistant for deciding which roles deserve your limited application time. This demo shows a synthetic candidate and original fictional jobs. It separates required and preferred qualifications, connects matches to evidence, displays deterministic Fit Score mathematics, and uses GPT-5.6 Structured Outputs for a grounded application strategy.

Built with Codex and GPT-5.6 for OpenAI Build Week, Apps for Your Life.

Fit Score is an evidence-based ranking score, not a hiring probability. No application is submitted by the product.
