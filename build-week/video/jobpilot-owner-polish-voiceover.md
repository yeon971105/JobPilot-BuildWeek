# JobPilot Owner Polish RC1 Voiceover

Job search fatigue is not just finding openings. It is searching separate sites, rereading requirements, comparing your experience, and deciding whether another application deserves the next hour.

JobPilot starts with one promise: find the roles worth your time. For You narrows the synthetic demo to three focused decisions, with concise reasons based on evidence fit, stated preferences, distance, and freshness.

Opening a role leads with the conclusion. The Fit Score is an integer, the fit conclusion is plain language, and Apply Priority, strongest evidence, attention areas, relevant experience, practical fit, and the original destination are visible without technical noise.

Application Strategy answers the next human questions: should I act, why, what should I emphasize, what should I prepare, and what should I clarify? The default review is concise. Supporting evidence is translated into capability and experience names. Provenance and limitations stay collapsed until requested. This is prepared GPT-5.6 work in Codex, not a live response, and it never changes the deterministic score.

Evidence remains available on demand. The grouped view separates strong support, partial support, missing evidence, and items needing review. Exact role and candidate excerpts can be expanded without exposing internal identifiers in the primary interface.

Score Proof visualizes the frozen one-hundred-point contract. Exact arithmetic remains in the Technical Ledger, while the user-facing decision stays readable.

Every Score Receipt is independently verifiable. The verifier reproduces canonical hashing, micro-point arithmetic, caps, transfers, evidence checks, and rounding. Hidden adjustments remain zero.

When a role is worth pursuing, JobPilot opens the original employer destination deliberately. This public demo uses a fictional employer page. There is no form, resume transmission, redirect, or automatic submission.

Saving a role updates only browser-local planning state. The tracker supports Saved, Interested, Preparing, and Applied. Applied is a planning label only; JobPilot never auto-applies.

Gemma 4 12B is the primary semantic model in the local architecture. Deterministic code calculates every Fit Score. GPT-5.6 Sol review was prepared through Codex. OpenAI API requests and cost are zero. Codex helped reproduce the baseline, repair comprehension defects, and certify the release across desktop and mobile.

Fit Score is not a hiring probability, and no human-study result is claimed without authentic final participants. JobPilot connects discovery, evidence, proof, employer handoff, and private tracking so people can spend less time decoding job posts and more time acting on roles that fit.
