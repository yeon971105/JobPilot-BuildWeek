# Authentic directional usability evidence

**Status:** `AUTHENTIC_DIRECTIONAL_USABILITY_EVIDENCE`  
**Evidence label:** Moderated directional usability study, n=5

In a moderated directional usability study with five participants, the JobPilot condition had a median task time of 18 seconds versus 39 seconds for the traditional condition. Factual accuracy was 100% versus 75%, while average confidence increased from 3.8 to 6.0 out of 7 and clarity increased from 3.6 to 6.2 out of 7.

All participants completed the traditional condition first and JobPilot second. Different roles prevented same-role carryover, but practice or order effects may contribute to the observed difference.

This is small directional usability evidence, not causal proof, population inference, hiring-outcome calibration, or evidence of interview or offer success. No confidence interval or statistical-significance test is reported.
